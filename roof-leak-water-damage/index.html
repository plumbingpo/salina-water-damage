Dealing With Roof Leak Water Damage in Salina

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. Often the attic has been damp for some time before any brown mark reaches the room below. Locating all of the moisture and drying it is our job.

We are a water damage crew, not a roofing company. Our job is the inside of the house: pulling out soaked insulation, drying the framing and drywall, treating for mold and putting the ceiling back together. Should the leak still be active, our crew can cover that section with a tarp for now, or time our work with the roofing contractor you pick so nothing gets sealed over an open leak.

First Five Minutes

Before We Arrive

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Contain the WaterPut a pan or bucket beneath each drip, and shift rugs, electronics and furniture clear of the wet area. Lay towels or plastic sheeting down to protect the floor.
  3. Relieve a Ceiling BulgeIf the ceiling is holding a pocket of water, stand to one side and poke a small hole at the lowest point so it drains into a bucket.
  4. Leave the Roof AloneDo not climb onto a wet roof or crawl through a dark attic. Just observe the entry point from the room below, then hand the job to a trained crew.
  5. Photograph the DamageTake photos and video of the ceiling, the attic and any damaged belongings before you move or throw anything away, then call us to start drying.
Why It Happens

What Causes It

  • Corroded or loose metal flashing where the roof meets a chimney, a vent or a wall.
  • Water backing up at the eave and under the shingles because the gutters are blocked.
  • Leaves and grit collecting where two roof slopes meet, damming rain until it seeps through.
  • Skylight seals and curbs that dry out, crack and let rain run inside.
  • Holes and fractures left in the roof after a branch or hailstones hit it.
If It Is Left

Where the Damage Spreads

Drywall Ceilings

Staining, sagging and loss of strength follow when drywall is not dried. Joints open, tape peels, and a waterlogged piece may fall without much notice.

Rafters and Decking

Wood in the roof structure that is soaked over and over darkens, swells and softens. Decay here means less strength in what carries the roof.

Hidden Attic Mold

Damp framing, paper-faced drywall, warmth and darkness give mold what it needs. Within 24 to 48 hours it can start growing, then move across the bottom of the roof deck.

Ceiling Fixtures and Wiring

Because fixtures are holes in the drywall, a leak often shows up at a light or fan first. Wet connections can short or corrode, so the circuit should stay off until it is checked.

Insurance

Does Insurance Cover It?

Homeowners policies often cover interior water damage when a roof leak is sudden and accidental, such as wind tearing off shingles or a limb puncturing the roof. Leaks blamed on age, wear or skipped maintenance are usually denied, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the interior. We document the damage with photos and moisture readings for your adjuster, but coverage depends on your policy, so read it or ask your agent.

Pricing

How the Quote Is Built

  • Whether wet attic insulation can be dried in place, and how large an area it covers.
  • Damage beyond the ceiling, such as wet floors, wall cavities or furniture.
  • Mold, if present, and the number of days or weeks water was getting in.
  • How many square feet of wall and ceiling drywall have to be removed.

What We Do

What Happens After You Call

1

Trace the Leak Path

Moisture meters and a thermal camera let us follow the rain through the attic, the ceilings and the walls, so spots that look dry from underneath are not missed.

2

Tarp and Protect

If the roof is still open to the weather, we can install a temporary tarp or coordinate with your roofer. Anything sitting beneath the damage, including the flooring, gets protected too.

3

Tear Out and Dry

We pull out insulation and drywall that cannot be saved, then run air movers and dehumidifiers in the attic and the rooms below, checking moisture readings until the framing is dry.

4

Sanitize and Repair

Exposed wood gets an antimicrobial treatment where needed. With the roof fixed, our team installs insulation and drywall, finishes the joints, and primes and paints so the repair blends in.
