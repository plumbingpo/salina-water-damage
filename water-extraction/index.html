Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. For emergency water extraction in Salina, Salina Restoration brings truck-mounted units and portable extractors built to pull out hundreds of gallons every minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Find the main shutoff valve for the house and put a clear label on it
  • Maintain appliance supply lines and replace hoses that show wear or age
  • Consider an automatic shutoff valve, which senses a leak and closes the water supply
  • Raise anything you store in the basement a few inches above the floor
  • Critical areas of the house should each have a leak sensor that you know is working
  • Pour water into the sump pit a few times a year to confirm the pump kicks on

What a Professional Brings to Water Extraction

The speed and thoroughness of water extraction directly determine the extent of damage to your property and the cost of restoration. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. This speed difference is not just about convenience, it is about preventing thousands of dollars in additional damage that occurs with every hour water stays in contact with building materials.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. We go after those hidden deposits with specialty tools and methods, and that takes out far more water than extracting the surface only. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.

Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Seams in the floor that ooze water under foot pressure
  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well
  • Storm water collecting in the lowest level of the home
  • Saturated carpet that squishes underfoot or shows water pooling
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • Ceilings, light fixtures or walls that are actively leaking water
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
