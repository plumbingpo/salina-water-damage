Home / Services / Burst Pipe Repair

A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. The trigger may be a hard freeze, corroded or aging lines, high pressure or physical damage, but the need is the same: act immediately so water does less harm to the structure, the floors, your possessions and the building's systems.

Day or night, Salina Restoration responds to burst pipe emergencies across Salina and the nearby communities. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Burst pipes often damage multiple rooms and multiple levels, because water follows gravity through wall cavities, floor assemblies and ceiling spaces. Our thermal imaging and moisture detection equipment traces water migration paths so every affected area is identified and properly dried, which prevents the mold growth and structural deterioration that hidden moisture causes.

How to Tell You Need Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • No water at a tap in freezing weather, a sign the line is frozen
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • Water charges that jump for no clear reason, a sign of a concealed leak
  • Wet spots, bulging or staining on walls or ceilings
  • Water pooling on floors with no apparent source
  • Visible water spraying or dripping from exposed pipes

Why Professional Burst Pipe Repair Matters

Burst pipe water damage often extends far beyond what is visible on the surface. It moves between ceiling joists, inside floor assemblies, beside electrical conduit and down wall cavities, soaking materials you might not find for days or even weeks. Hidden spaces may hold established mold by the time you notice, with structural materials seriously weakened as well. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. A truck-mounted unit clears water at hundreds of times the speed of a shop vac, while a commercial dehumidifier takes 10 to 20 times more moisture out of the air than a household model. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and how to operate it quickly
  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • Outdoor faucets should be closed, drained and free of hoses ahead of freezing weather
  • Install water leak detection sensors near water heaters and main supply lines
  • Have a licensed plumber inspect your plumbing system every few years for wear and corrosion
  • Leave the heat on when you travel in winter so indoor pipes stay above freezing
