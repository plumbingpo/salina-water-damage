Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Salina Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Salina, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Salina

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • Wastewater seeping from the sewer cleanout or pooling in the yard above the line
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else

What a Professional Brings to Sewage Cleanup

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Do not wait on a slow drain, which can turn into a full blockage
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • Have roots removed where trees grow by the sewer, before they get inside the pipe and plug it
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects

Hazard Check and Containment
